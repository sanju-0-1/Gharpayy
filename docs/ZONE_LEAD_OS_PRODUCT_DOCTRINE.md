# Gharpayy Zone Lead OS — Product Doctrine

## One sentence

WhatsApp tells us what the customer is actually doing. CRM tells us what Gharpayy is doing. Zone Lead OS continuously compares the two and forces the next best action until the customer is resolved.

## Golden flow

Zone → one primary WhatsApp account → four screenshot syncs per day → zero-miss observation reconciliation → one permanent Zone Lead Ledger → Draft 30 → Active 13 → claim before call → full customer context → activity/outcome → owner + dated next action → role handoff → tour → quote → payment → booking → verified physical check-in → continuous WhatsApp re-sync.

## Non-negotiable invariants

1. A repeated screenshot creates another observation, never another customer.
2. Every visible WhatsApp row is either linked to one canonical customer, explicitly marked non-customer with reason, or remains a visible review exception.
3. Screenshot truth, parsed full-chat truth, and CRM human activity remain separate evidence layers.
4. One customer can have only one current Draft reservation / live work claim.
5. Mark/claim first; call second. An operator may not silently work a customer owned by another person.
6. Drafting is a view over the shared Zone Lead Ledger, never a separate lead database.
7. Draft 30 is the portfolio; Active 13 is only the rolling execution window.
8. Every unresolved open customer has an owner, next action, and due time—or appears as leakage.
9. WhatsApp message intelligence is advisory. It cannot manufacture quotation, booking, lost, or checked-in evidence.
10. Payment and check-in are separate. CHECKED_IN requires verified payment, required owner approval, arrival, room/bed allocation, KYC, agreement, and keys.
11. Flow Ops, TCM, Closing, Ops, Control Tower and Admin move through roles on the same customer record, not separate CRMs.
12. RED metrics must drill into the exact customers causing the leak.

## Operating objective

The system cannot guarantee that every customer books. It must guarantee process coverage: every valid customer is booked, explicitly lost with reason, intentionally waiting/dormant until a date, or has an accountable owner plus a dated next action.
